Neon Quiver

A glowing 3D arrow logic puzzle for iPhone. Tap an arrow, slide it free, and clear the whole shape.

FAQ

Restore purchases: open the Shop tab at the bottom of the screen and tap Restore purchases (also available in Settings: the gear icon on Home, or Profile → Settings). Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.
Remove ads: buy "Remove Ads" in the shop; rewarded videos for extra lives stay optional.
Progress: saved on your device.
