Neon Quiver – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Neon Quiver ("the app") is developed by Jalal Najafi. This policy explains what information the app uses.

No account, no personal data collected by us

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location on our own servers. Game progress is stored on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the Advertising Identifier / IDFA if you allow tracking), coarse location derived from IP address, ad interaction data, and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. On iOS you are asked for permission via App Tracking Transparency; if you decline, ads are not personalized based on tracking. In the EEA/UK a consent message may be shown. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as Remove Ads and the Skin Pack) are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details; the app only receives confirmation of what you purchased.

Camera and microphone

This version of the app does not use the camera. Optional clip recording uses Apple ReplayKit and is only shared if you choose to share it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change.
